Entries proceed through the following state changes:
`stub` → `in-research` ↔  `research-review` →`draft` → `review` → `complete`

**stub** - incomplete placeholder of intent, not a decision or enough info yet to proceed to research

**research** - there's enough metadata about the track to definitively identify it and collect research and the initial research needs done, or there are open research questions to investigate

**research-review** - a round of research has been completed and is ready for review

**draft** - the results of the research have been used to create a draft entry in some stage of completeness

**review** - a candidate draft entry is ready for seasoning time and final editorial review

**complete** - an entry is ready to publish
