OK, things are about to get a bit weird, but you'll just have to bear with me on this one. I promise you the juice is worth the squeeze. 

At eighteen minutes, today's track, "Blues (A Folk Tale)" takes up the entire first side of the *Harlem Bush Music - Uru*, and as you'll very quickly find out, not all of it is funky. If you keep an open mind and don't turn it off though, you'll be rewarded with possibly the best example of free funk that's ever been offered up. As a bonus, if you don't mind a good country blues, free jazz, or modern blues, those are in here too.
