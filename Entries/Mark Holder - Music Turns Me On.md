### Writing Theme Suggestions

- **"The Guyanese soul man who had to be in Jamaica."** Mark Holder was a Guyanese (Georgetown-born) reggae/soul pioneer, but this LP was cut in Kingston at Harry J's and Dynamic Sounds with an A-list of Jamaican session players (Tyrone Downie, Cedric Brooks, Mikey Chung, Val Douglas). The entry can hang on the gap between where an artist is *from* and where the *funk* got made.
- **"Funk hidden inside a lovers-rock LP."** Discogs files the album under Reggae / Lovers Rock / Roots Reggae, yet collectors prize it almost entirely for one "island funk bomb" — "Music Turns Me On." A theme about the single deep-funk cut buried on an otherwise reggae record.
- **"The blog-era rediscovery."** This is a record kept alive not by labels or charts but by record-digger blogs, popsike auctions, and YouTube rips — a case study in how obscure 1970s Caribbean funk survives via crate-digging culture.

### Listening Mission Suggestions

- This is the one heavy funk cut on an album the experts file as lovers-rock reggae. Listen for the moment the rhythm section locks into a straight funk pocket rather than a reggae one-drop — what makes it read as funk and not reggae?
- The band is a who's-who of Jamaican session players (Tyrone Downie on keys, Cedric Brooks on sax). Pick out the organ and the horn line and ask whether they sound like Kingston or like a North-American soul session.
- Find the "Mark Wayward Edit" afterward and compare it to the 1974 original. What did the modern editor stretch, loop, or cut to turn an album track into a dancefloor edit?

### Interest and Surprise

#### Track
- "Music Turns Me On" is the prize of the LP for funk collectors — auction listings call it the "MASSIVE funk track" / "island funk bomb," and one seller noted that even on a heat-damaged copy "the BIG track on this record 'Music Turns Me On' is completely intact." — Source: https://www.popsike.com/MARK-HOLDER-TODAY-TOMORROW-AND-ALWAYS-LP-ISLAND-FUNK-BOMB-DERVIA-HEAR-IT/261475214697.html
- It was compiled onto the (unofficial) crate-digger compilation *Funk Power Black Power* (Soul Patrol Records, 2001, France), evidence of its standing in the rare-funk reissue underground. — Source: https://www.discogs.com/release/820358-Various-Funk-Power-Black-Power
- A modern dancefloor re-edit, the "Mark Wayward Edit," exists and circulates on SoundCloud / Last.fm, showing the track's continued life among DJs. — Source: https://www.last.fm/music/Mark+Holder/_/Music+Turns+Me+On+(Mark+Wayward+Edit)

#### Album
- Despite the funk reputation of its standout track, Discogs classifies the album as Reggae / Funk/Soul with styles Lovers Rock and Roots Reggae. — Source: https://www.discogs.com/release/3305965-Mark-Holder-Today-Tomorrow-And-Always
- It was recorded at two of Jamaica's most storied studios — Harry J's Recording Studio and Dynamic Sounds — and engineered by Sylvan Morris, with Mark Holder himself credited as producer and arranger. — Source: https://www.discogs.com/release/3305965-Mark-Holder-Today-Tomorrow-And-Always
- The personnel is a Kingston dream team: Tyrone Downie (later of Bob Marley & The Wailers) on organ/piano, Cedric Brooks on saxophone, Mikey Chung and Clarence Wears on guitar, and Val Douglas on bass. — Source: https://www.discogs.com/release/3305965-Mark-Holder-Today-Tomorrow-And-Always

#### Artist
- Mark Holder was born in Georgetown, (British) Guiana, in 1947 and grew up in the Lodge area; he trained as a woodworker and worked as a government clerk before music took over. — Source: https://www.stabroeknews.com/2010/05/29/the-scene/mark-holder-still-%E2%80%98something-of-value%E2%80%99/
- He was billed as one of the first Caribbean artists to sign with Atlantic and Capitol Records, and over a 30-year career released 500+ songs and ~20 albums; he is best known for "Something of Value." — Source: https://www.stabroeknews.com/2010/05/29/the-scene/mark-holder-still-%E2%80%98something-of-value%E2%80%99/
- Over his career he shared stages with Bob Marley, Eddy Grant, Aretha Franklin, Mighty Sparrow and the Jackson Five, and worked with Peter Tosh, Ken Lazarus and Jackie Opel. — Source: https://www.stabroeknews.com/2010/05/29/the-scene/mark-holder-still-%E2%80%98something-of-value%E2%80%99/
- He died on Thursday, July 29, 2021, aged 74, in Canada. — Source: https://demerarawaves.com/2021/07/29/guyanese-singing-icon-mark-holder-dies/
