### Writing Theme Suggestions

- **"The two-minute mark where the song explodes."** Every source fixates on the same structural trick: "Stay Together" opens slow, heavy and almost sleepy with a sing-song part, then detonates into hard, fast funk. A theme built on that hinge — the moment a soul ballad becomes a funk stomper — frames the whole entry. Sources: http://mooimuziek.blogspot.com/2009/05/exclusive-interview-with-clay-drayton.html, https://kaydeerecords.com/products/kd-010-soul-excitement-stay-together
- **"A one-single band that vanished into greatness."** Soul Excitement cut exactly one 45 and dissolved in early 1970 — yet its leader Clay Drayton went on to arrange Diana Ross's "Love Hangover" and write for the Jackson 5 and the Supremes. The angle: obscurity and stardom separated by a single record. Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clay_Drayton, http://mooimuziek.blogspot.com/2009/05/exclusive-interview-with-clay-drayton.html
- **"Rescued by the crates."** A record that sold nowhere in 1969 became a five-figure collector grail, canonized by Quantic's "World's Rarest Funk 45s" and reissued by Kenny Dope's Kay-Dee label — the textbook deep-funk arc of delayed recognition. Sources: https://jazzmanfunkcompilations.bandcamp.com/album/quantic-presents-the-worlds-rarest-funk-45s, https://kaydeerecords.com/products/kd-010-soul-excitement-stay-together

### Listening Mission Suggestions

- The track begins as a slow, hypnotic sing-song. Note the exact moment the band drops the disguise and the drums kick into double-time — how long does the fake-out last before it explodes?
- Ignore the melody for one pass and follow only the drums. Once the tempo lifts, listen for why crate-digging DJs prized this break enough to make the record a collector's grail.
- Both this A-side energy and the "Stay Together" lyric are "super-righteous." Listen for how the vocal preaches togetherness while the rhythm section refuses to sit still — do the words and the groove agree with each other?

### Interest and Surprise

#### Track
- "Stay Together" is credited to A.C. Drummer Jr., Clay Drayton and Roland Washington; the flip "Smile" was written by Clay Drayton, Paul Cutner and Tami Lester Smith. Both leads were sung by Tamy Smith and Clay Drayton. — Source: https://www.discogs.com/release/2767780-Soul-Excitement-Smile-Stay-Together
- The single is a genuine collector's rarity — it turns up on rare-funk want lists and auction sites (popsike) and was reissued 7" on Kenny Dope's Kay-Dee Records (KD-010). — Source: https://kaydeerecords.com/products/kd-010-soul-excitement-stay-together
- It was canonized on "Quantic Presents... The World's Rarest Funk 45s" (Jazzman, 2006), track 9. — Source: https://jazzmanfunkcompilations.bandcamp.com/album/quantic-presents-the-worlds-rarest-funk-45s
- The B-side "Smile" is described by collectors as a deep soul number that "almost sounds like a lost treasure from the vault of Stax Records." — Source: https://kaydeerecords.com/products/kd-010-soul-excitement-stay-together

#### Album
- No album exists. The band recorded only this one 45 for Pink Dolphin. — Source: http://mooimuziek.blogspot.com/2009/05/exclusive-interview-with-clay-drayton.html

#### Artist
- Soul Excitement was a psychedelic-funk group from Newark, New Jersey, formed in 1968 by bassist/frontman Clarence "Clay" Drayton and guitarist A.C. Drummer Jr., who had previously played together in Cecil Garrett and the Fascinations. — Source: http://mooimuziek.blogspot.com/2009/05/exclusive-interview-with-clay-drayton.html
- Pink Dolphin Records was a vanity label: a couple who liked the band paid for the studio time and the pressing. The group gigged nightclubs every weekend for roughly four years under that arrangement. — Source: http://mooimuziek.blogspot.com/2009/05/exclusive-interview-with-clay-drayton.html
- The band appeared live (alongside the Grand Prix Machine vocal group) on Channel 13's "Soul!," the pioneering Black-culture TV program hosted by Ellis Haizlip, in fall 1968. — Source: http://mooimuziek.blogspot.com/2009/05/exclusive-interview-with-clay-drayton.html
- Soul Excitement dissolved around January/February 1970 when Drayton and A.C. Drummer left to tour full-time with Dee Dee Warwick. — Source: http://mooimuziek.blogspot.com/2009/05/exclusive-interview-with-clay-drayton.html
- Clay Drayton (b. August 4, 1947) went on to a decades-long career at Motown as a songwriter/arranger/producer — most notably arranging Diana Ross's gold hit "Love Hangover" — with compositions recorded by the Jackson 5, the Supremes, High Inergy, Táta Vega and Bette Midler. — Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clay_Drayton

### Genre Suggestions

- **Deep Funk (recommended).** This is the textbook case: a regional private-press 45 that sold poorly in 1969, was overlooked for decades, then rediscovered by crate-digging DJs — headlining Quantic's "World's Rarest Funk 45s" and getting a Kay-Dee (Kenny Dope) reissue. The book defines deep funk precisely as "delayed recognition." Sources: https://jazzmanfunkcompilations.bandcamp.com/album/quantic-presents-the-worlds-rarest-funk-45s , https://kaydeerecords.com/products/kd-010-soul-excitement-stay-together
- **Classic Funk.** Released 1969, right at the start of the book's 1969–1977 classic era, with funk as the track's primary identity once it kicks in. It falls short of the "commercial pipeline / radio play / chart presence" part of the classic-funk definition, so deep funk is the better fit — but the sound itself is core classic-funk vocabulary. Source: On Subgenres.md definitions
- **Psychedelic Funk.** Multiple sources describe Soul Excitement as a "psychedelic funk band," and the slow, "tripped-out" sing-song intro supports that reading before the track hardens into straight funk. A defensible secondary tag. Source: http://mooimuziek.blogspot.com/2009/05/exclusive-interview-with-clay-drayton.html

### Region Suggestions

- **US - Northeast (recommended).** Soul Excitement formed and was based in Newark, New Jersey. The region enumeration in Field Values.md has no New Jersey (or Mid-Atlantic) entry, and Newark sits firmly in the Northeast, so US - Northeast is the correct closest valid value. Source: http://mooimuziek.blogspot.com/2009/05/exclusive-interview-with-clay-drayton.html
- **US - New York (alternate, weaker).** The band's notable TV appearance was on New York's WNET/Channel 13 "Soul!", and it operated in the greater NYC metro orbit — but the group itself was a New Jersey act, so New York would misstate its origin.
