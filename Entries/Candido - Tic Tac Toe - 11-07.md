Long before TikTok, kids entertained themselves with tic-tac-toe. In 1963, the Stax house band of Booker T. Jones, Steve Cropper, Al Jackson Jr., and Donald “Duck” Dunn named a B-side instrumental “Tic Tac Toe.” Eight years later, Havana-born conga player Candido Camero Guerra recorded a funky Latin arrangement of the same tune for Blue Note in New York.

By the early 1970s, the renowned jazz label Blue Note was making jazz-funk crossover records, and his *Beautiful* album was part of that transition. His biggest success came in 1979 when he went full Latin disco on *Dancin’ & Prancin’* for the Salsoul label.

In traditional settings, multiple congueros would each play a single conga. Candido is often credited as the first to use multiple tuned congas in a small-group jazz setup for one player, a style that modern conga players take for granted. A 2006 documentary, *Candido: Hands of Fire*, helped bring him wider attention, and he received a Latin Grammy Lifetime Achievement Award in 2009. Candido died in New York City on November 7, 2020, at the age of 99.

### Interest and Surprise

#### Track

- "Tic Tac Toe" is a cover of a Booker T. & the M.G.'s instrumental originally released as the B-side of "Mo' Onions" in November 1963, written by Booker T. Jones, Steve Cropper, Donald "Duck" Dunn, and Al Jackson, Jr. — Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beautiful_(Candido_Camero_album), https://www.discogs.com/release/2916121-Booker-T-The-MGs-Mo-Onions-Tic-Tac-Toe
- Candido's version runs 4:03 and was cut at A&R Studios in NYC during sessions on Oct 20 and Oct 27, 1970. — Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beautiful_(Candido_Camero_album)
- WhoSampled does not list any samples of Candido's "Tic Tac Toe," so despite its heavy deep-funk-comp pedigree it appears to have escaped the sampling pipeline. — Source: https://www.whosampled.com/Candido/ (absence)

#### Album

- *Beautiful* was produced and arranged by Joe Cain, who would later produce Candido's 1979 Salsoul breakout *Dancin' & Prancin'*. — Source: https://www.sessiondays.com/2023/01/1971-candido-beautiful/, https://5mag.net/news/candido-camero-musician-conga-disco/
- The personnel is a deep NYC session roster: trumpeters Bernie Glow and Pat Russo, trombonist Alan Raph, reedman Joe Grimm, guitarist David Spinozza, bassist Richard Davis (on most tracks), drummer Herbie Lovelle, keys by Frank Anderson. Cover photography by Joel Brodsky (who shot the iconic Doors "Young Lion" session). — Source: https://www.sessiondays.com/2023/01/1971-candido-beautiful/
- AllMusic: three stars. Matt Collar calls it not essential Candido but "should hold a fascination for funk-jazz enthusiasts." Penguin Guide to Jazz also three stars. — Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beautiful_(Candido_Camero_album)
- Reissued by Soul Jazz Records' Sounds of the Universe imprint, signaling its place in crate-digger canon. — Source: https://soundsoftheuniverse.com/sjr/product/candido-beautiful-1970

#### Artist

- Cándido Camero Guerra, born April 22, 1921 in Havana, died November 7, 2020 in NYC at age 99. — Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C%C3%A1ndido_Camero
- He began playing homemade bongos at age 4. His father taught him tres (Cuban guitar) before he moved to percussion. Played professionally in Cuba from age 14. — Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C%C3%A1ndido_Camero
- Moved to New York in 1946. Went on to play with Dizzy Gillespie, Billy Taylor, Stan Kenton, Tony Bennett and countless others. — Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C%C3%A1ndido_Camero
- Widely credited as the first high-profile congero to play multiple tuned congas simultaneously in a jazz combo, and to build custom multi-percussion rigs with foot-operated cowbells and mounted güiros. This is the setup every modern conguero inherits. — Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C%C3%A1ndido_Camero, https://pas.org/candido-camero/
- NEA Jazz Master (2008); Latin Grammy Lifetime Achievement Award (2009). — Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C%C3%A1ndido_Camero
- His biggest commercial moment came later with 1979's Salsoul cuts "Jingo" and "Thousand Finger Man," Paradise Garage staples and early-disco/proto-house touchstones. — Source: https://5mag.net/news/candido-camero-musician-conga-disco/
- A 2006 documentary, *Candido: Hands of Fire*, chronicled his career. — Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C%C3%A1ndido_Camero
