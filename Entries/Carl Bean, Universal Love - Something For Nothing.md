### Writing Theme Suggestions

- "The funk pastor before the pulpit" — A 2:05 funk-gospel jolt recorded by a man who, one year later, founded the LGBTQ-affirming Unity Fellowship Church and went on to lead a pioneering Black AIDS-activism movement. The track is a snapshot of Carl Bean as artist before Carl Bean as minister and activist.
- "Erasing the line between R&B and gospel" — Bean himself framed Universal Love as part of a movement to dissolve the boundary between sacred and secular Black music. Use the track as a case study in what gospel funk actually sounds like in practice.
- "The album Motown noticed" — *All We Need Is Love* flopped commercially, yet it put Bean on Motown's radar and led to "I Was Born This Way" (1977). A theme about a failed record that quietly changed a life.

### Listening Mission Suggestions

- This track clocks in at just 2 minutes 5 seconds, the shortest song on the album. Listen for how Bean packs a full gospel-funk message into that tiny window. What gets cut to make it fit?
- The title track and "Gotta Be Some Change" got the single push, not this one. Play "Something For Nothing" back to back with "Gotta Be Some Change" and decide which one you'd have released as the A-side, and why.
- Bean would become a preacher within a year. Listen for the pulpit in his phrasing here. Where does the funk singer end and the future minister begin?

### Interest and Surprise

#### Track

- "Something For Nothing" is the shortest cut on the album at 2:05, sandwiched on side B between "Yes, Thank You Lord" and "Redeemed" — Source: https://www.discogs.com/master/969973-Carl-Bean-And-Universal-Love-3-All-We-Need-Is-Love
- Carl Bean wrote all the songs on the album, including this one; it is described as a "funky message song" — Source: https://jointzoftheday.substack.com/p/carl-bean-may-26-1944-sept-7-2021-born-this-way
- The track was never released as a single and exists only as album track B3 — Source: https://api.discogs.com/database/search?artist=Carl+Bean&type=release&format=7%22 (only 1974 Peacock 7" was "Gotta Be Some Change / Redeemed")

#### Album

- *All We Need Is Love* (1974) was released on Peacock Records (cat. PLP 59208 / ABC Peacock P-20006 series), the gospel label founded by Don Robey that had been sold to ABC Records in 1973 — Source: https://jointzoftheday.substack.com/p/carl-bean-may-26-1944-sept-7-2021-born-this-way
- Production credits: arranged by Arthur Wright, produced by Lee Young, design by Earl Klasky, photography by Ron Slenzak; lead vocals were shared, with Alahaundra Romeo Jr. soloing on "Gotta Be Some Change" and Jackie Cannon on "Redeemed" — Source: https://www.discogs.com/release/3159259-Carl-Bean-And-Universal-Love-All-We-Need-Is-Love
- The album sold poorly but is now a sought-after rare gospel-funk record (Discogs shows hundreds wanting it, few owning it; lowest asking price around $68) — Source: https://www.discogs.com/release/3159259-Carl-Bean-And-Universal-Love-All-We-Need-Is-Love
- Despite the album's commercial failure, it put Bean on Motown's radar — leading to his 1977 cover of "I Was Born This Way" — Source: https://jointzoftheday.substack.com/p/carl-bean-may-26-1944-sept-7-2021-born-this-way

#### Artist

- Carl Bean (b. May 26, 1944, Baltimore; d. September 7, 2021, Los Angeles) recorded the 1977 Motown disco anthem "I Was Born This Way," an early gay-liberation song that inspired Lady Gaga's 2011 album/single of the same name — Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Bean
- In 1982 Bean became active in AIDS advocacy and founded the Minority AIDS Project in Los Angeles, still operating today; he co-founded the National Minority AIDS Council in 1987 — Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Bean
- In 1985 Bean founded the Unity Fellowship Church Movement, a Protestant denomination welcoming LGBTQ+ African Americans (his first church work in LA began around 1975) — Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Bean
- Bean published his autobiography, also titled *I Was Born This Way*, in 2010 — Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Bean
